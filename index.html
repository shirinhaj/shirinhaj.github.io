Welcome!

I am a postdoctoral researcher in the Department of Computer Science and Engineering at the University of Bologna, where my research investigates how Extended Reality (XR), Artificial Intelligence (AI), and sensor technologies can be harnessed to augment human cognition. Positioned at the intersection of Human-Computer Interaction (HCI), cognitive science, and immersive systems, my work explores how intelligent and adaptive interfaces can support learning, communication, and everyday decision-making. My academic background spans multiple disciplines. I hold a PhD in Computer Science and Engineering from the University of Bologna, where my doctoral research focused on the design and implementation of cognitive augmentation systems using XR, AI, and sensors. Prior to this, I earned an MSc in Cognitive Science with a specialization in cognitive psychology and spatial navigation, and a BSc in Computer Science. Over the years, I have led and contributed to interdisciplinary research initiatives that combine immersive media, machine learning, and user-centered design. I have engaged in international collaborations—including a research visit to the University of Hamburg—and worked on projects funded by the European Union and regional institutions. My research has been presented at premier conferences such as IEEE VR, ISMAR, and SUI, and featured in peer-reviewed journals. I am particularly passionate about building and evaluating experimental systems—from conversational virtual agents to XR-powered digital twins—that promote accessibility, cognitive empowerment, and inclusive user experiences.
