I am a postdoctoral researcher in Computer Science and Engineering at the University of Bologna. I work at the intersection of Extended Reality (XR), Artificial Intelligence, and privacy-centric HCI, building and evaluating systems that augment cognition and support learning, communication, and everyday decision-making.

My background spans XR design and development, context‑aware agents, and sensor‑integrated interactive systems. I collaborate with researchers and students on projects funded by European and regional programs and my work appears in venues such as IEEE VR, ISMAR, and ACM SUI.

Prospective students interested in XR privacy, identity, or multimodal recommenders are welcome to email me with the subject line “XR Privacy Applicant.”

My Research

My work explores how intelligent, embodied interfaces can enhance human capabilities while preserving agency and privacy. Current directions include:

  • Copyright/Provenance & Generative AI: watermark‑resilient detectors, model‑level defenses, and post‑deployment forensics for T2I systems.
  • Security & Privacy of Deep Models: robust learning against obfuscation/identity leakage in Social VR; evaluation toolkits for avatar identifiability.
  • Mobile/XR Sensing for Privacy: consent‑aware interaction and selective embodiment; evaluating speech/gesture channels for privacy‑preserving collaboration.
