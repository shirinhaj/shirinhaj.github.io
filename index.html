Shirin Hajahmadi

I am a postdoctoral researcher in the Department of Computer Science and Engineering at the University of Bologna, conducting research at the intersection of Extended Reality (XR), Cognitive Augmentation, Human–Computer Interaction (HCI), Intelligent Systems, Human–AI Interaction, and privacy and security in immersive environments. I hold a Ph.D. in Computer Science and Engineering from the University of Bologna. Prior to this, I completed an M.Sc. in Cognitive Science as well as a B.Sc. in Computer Science. Over the years, I have conceived and developed interdisciplinary research initiatives that combine immersive media, and user-centered design. I have engaged in international collaborations and worked on projects funded by the European Union and regional institutions. My research has been presented at premier conferences and featured in peer-reviewed journals. I am particularly passionate about building and evaluating experimental systems—from conversational virtual agents to XR-powered digital twins—that promote accessibility, cognitive empowerment, and inclusive user experiences.

VARLab

The Virtual and Augmented Reality Laboratory (VARLab) at the University of Bologna is a multidisciplinary research group dedicated to investigating how immersive technologies reshape human cognition, interaction, and experience. Drawing from fields such as computer science, cognitive psychology, and interaction design, VARLab explores themes including virtual embodiment, multisensory feedback, and the ethical and social dimensions of extended reality (XR).

As a postdoctoral researcher affiliated with VARLab, my work lies at the intersection of extended reality (XR), artificial intelligence (AI), and human cognition. I investigate how immersive environments and intelligent systems can be designed to foster cognitive empowerment and meaningful interaction. My current research explores conversational agents for XR-based learning environments and the construction of privacy-preserving identities in Social VR.

Within VARLab, I contribute to interdisciplinary projects and collaborate with both local and international partners. I also mentor students across fields such as computer science and cognitive science.

Learn more about the lab at the official VARLab website.
