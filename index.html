About

I am a postdoctoral researcher in the Department of Computer Science and Engineering at the University of Bologna. I study how Extended Reality (XR), Artificial Intelligence, and sensors can augment human cognition and support learning, communication, and everyday decision-making.

My research is situated at the intersection of HCI, immersive systems, and privacy. I design and evaluate experimental systems—from conversational virtual agents to XR-powered digital twins—with an emphasis on accessibility and inclusive user experience.

Research

Current themes include:

  • Identity & Privacy in Social VR: avatar identifiability, obfuscation, and consent-aware interfaces.
  • Voice-augmented Visual Guidance: evaluating multimodal guidance (M4) in XR–DT training systems.
  • Context-aware Multimodal Recommenders: memory-aided assistants for photo+text archives.
