Welcome!

I am a postdoctoral researcher in the Department of Computer Science and Engineering at the University of Bologna, where I explore how Extended Reality (XR), Artificial Intelligence (AI), and sensor technologies can enhance human cognition. My research lies at the intersection of Human-Computer Interaction (HCI), cognitive science, and immersive systems. My academic journey bridges diverse disciplines. I hold a PhD in Computer Science and Engineering from the University of Bologna, where my thesis—Cognitive Augmentation Systems through Extended Reality, Artificial Intelligence, and Sensors. My prior training includes an MSc in Cognitive Science with a focus on cognitive psychology and spatial navigation, and a BSc in Computer Science. Throughout my career, I have led and contributed to interdisciplinary research projects that integrate immersive media, machine learning, and user-centered design. I have conducted international research visits, including at the University of Hamburg, and collaborated with institutions and industry partners on European-funded projects. My work has been presented at leading conferences such as IEEE VR, ISMAR, and SUI, and published in journals. I am passionate about designing experimental systems—from intelligent virtual agents to XR-powered digital twins—that empower learning, communication, and creative expression. I enjoy mentoring students and working in cross-disciplinary teams. My research practice combines theoretical exploration with practice-based studies to tackle emerging challenges in interaction design, identity representation, and inclusive technology.
