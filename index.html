Welcome!

I’m a postdoctoral researcher in Human-Computer Interaction (HCI) at the Department of Computer Science and Engineering, University of Bologna. My research lies at the intersection of Extended Reality (XR), Artificial Intelligence (AI), and cognitive science, with a particular focus on the design of systems that support cognitive augmentation, inclusive learning, and identity protection in immersive environments. With a multidisciplinary academic background—ranging from a PhD in Computer Science at the University of Bologna to a Master’s in Cognitive Psychology—I explore how XR and intelligent agents can transform the way we learn, communicate, and interact with digital environments. My doctoral work, Cognitive Augmentation Systems through Extended Reality, Artificial Intelligence, and Sensors, investigated how multimodal interfaces can empower users in procedural learning, social communication, and identity expression. I’ve led and collaborated on research projects across Italy, Germany, and Spain, including a research visit at the University of Hamburg. I’ve presented at leading conferences such as IEEE VR, ISMAR, SUI, and HCII, and contributed to peer-reviewed publications on embodied agents, generative AI in virtual fashion, and XR-powered digital twins for sequential instruction. Today, I am part of a research initiative funded by the European Union’s NextGenerationEU program, focusing on privacy and security paradigms in extended reality scenarios. I am working with interdisciplinary teams, mentoring students from diverse fields like computer science, psychology, and the arts. In my research, I blend methods from HCI, machine learning, interaction design, and cognitive psychology. I combine theoretical exploration with empirical evaluations—designing and testing novel XR systems that address real-world challenges in education, accessibility, and digital identity. Feel free to explore my publications, research collaborations, or ongoing projects. I'm always happy to connect with students, researchers, and collaborators who share an interest in building human-centered and socially responsible immersive technologies.
